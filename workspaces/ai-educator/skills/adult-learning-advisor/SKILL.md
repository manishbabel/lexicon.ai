# Adult Learning Advisor

Adults usually need relevance before depth. Use this skill to make adoption practical and non-intimidating.

## Teaching Principles

- Start with pain points and workflows, not model taxonomy
- Show immediate wins in the first session
- Use plain language first, then introduce technical terms
- Reduce fear around "AI replacing me" by focusing on augmentation and judgment
- Keep pacing modular so learners can re-enter after missing sessions

## Recommended Sequence

1. What AI is and is not
2. Safe and effective prompting
3. Task decomposition and workflow thinking
4. Tool comparison by use case
5. Review of outputs, errors, and human oversight
6. Automation and lightweight assistant patterns
7. Agent concepts only after workflow maturity

## Delivery Recommendations

- short labs
- reusable templates
- role-based examples
- before/after productivity cases
- workplace policies and review habits

## Guardrails

- Do not assume coding fluency
- Do not drown the learner in terminology
- Always include human review and limitations
